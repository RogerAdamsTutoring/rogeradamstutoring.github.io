Roger Adams

Science tutor with over 30 years of experience in the classroom.

Exeter, United Kingdom

I am a science teacher with more than 30 years of experience and a great record of student achievement. I have recently retired after a long career in a comprehensive school, where I taught separate Biology, Chemistry, and Physics GCSEs as well as Combined Science GCSE. Many of my students have gone on to study sciences at top universities across the UK.

I can offer in-person tutoring at your home in and around Exeter, tailored to your child. My strengths are in building confidence and a can-do attitude so that success is fully owned by the student. Using mental models and stories, with a good dose of humour, I help students to tackle problems independently and communicate clearly for themselves.

I can support the Edexcel, AQA, and OCR science GCSE specifications.
